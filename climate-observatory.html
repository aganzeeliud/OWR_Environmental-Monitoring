Methodology & Data Sources

Scientific Approach

This observatory utilizes a multi-source integration approach, combining satellite observations with reanalysis models to provide a comprehensive view of the Okapi Wildlife Reserve's climate evolution.

  • ERA5-Land: High-resolution climate reanalysis for temperature and atmospheric variables.
  • CHIRPS Rainfall: 0.05° resolution precipitation data for precise local monitoring.
  • Anomalies: Calculated against a 20-year baseline (1990–2010) to identify deviations from historical norms.
  • Trend Analysis: Linear regression applied to decadal data to project warming and precipitation shifts.
