Scientific Overview

Project Sentinel employs a Multi-Sensor Fusion (MSF) approach to monitoring the Okapi Wildlife Reserve. By integrating thermal anomalies from NASA FIRMS (VIIRS 375m) with optical change detection from ESA Sentinel-2 and the Hansen Global Forest Change dataset, we achieve a high-fidelity temporal reconstruction of ecosystem flux.

Our methodology focuses on the Ituri River Basin, where artisanal mining and agricultural expansion represent the primary drivers of primary forest loss. The platform normalizes heterogeneous data sources into a unified temporal coordinate system (2000–2030), allowing for cross-validation between industrial permits (CAMI) and observed in-situ degradation.

The Congo Basin Comparison module leverages NASA GEDI (Global Ecosystem Dynamics Investigation) LiDAR data to quantify aboveground biomass density. This allows us to benchmark the OWR core zone against regional carbon sequestration averages, highlighting its status as one of the most critical high-carbon sinks in Central Africa.

Validation is performed monthly using high-resolution Planet NICFI imagery to ensure detection accuracy in high-cloud-cover equatorial regions.
