The Methodology

Advanced multi-criteria classification and severity modeling for wildfire dynamics in the Okapi Wildlife Reserve (2001-2026).

01

Fire Type Classification

Fires are classified into 9 distinct drivers using spatial proximity analysis, temporal recurrence, and spectral intensity indicators.

Slash-and-Burn

Detected in areas within 5km of villages with high forest loss intensity (>3ha) and high seasonality.

Mining-Related

Events intersecting or proximal to CAMI mining titles and IPIS artisanal site buffers.

Wildfire

Remote events (>10km from human features) with Extreme severity and dry season timing (Dec-Feb).

Charcoal Production

High forest loss events proximal to road corridors and transport hubs.

02

Severity Thresholds

Class FRP (MW) Burn Size (ha) Impact
Low < 30 < 2.0 Surface fire, low canopy impact
Moderate 30 - 60 2.0 - 4.0 Mixed intensity, understory loss
High 60 - 100 4.0 - 5.5 Significant crown impact
Extreme > 100 > 5.5 Complete stand replacement
04

Regional Benchmarking (Congo Basin)

Comparative analysis uses the broader Congo Basin as a baseline to evaluate OWR performance. Data is normalized per 1,000 hectares to ensure statistical relevance.

Carbon Sink Valuation

Carbon density (tC/ha) is derived from NASA GEDI L4A Footprint Level Aboveground Biomass Density and ESA CCI Biomass v3.0.

Regional Baseline

The regional deforestation baseline (0.6%/yr) is calculated from the Central Africa Forest Observatory (OFAC) 2024 State of the Forest report.

05

Biodiversity Inventory & Habitat Risk

Species counts and population estimates are aggregated from multi-annual field surveys (transect counts, camera traps) and IUCN Red List data. Habitat risk is modeled by intersecting forest loss buffers with core habitat zones.

Population Modeling

Estimates for Okapi and Chimpanzees use distance sampling methodology calibrated with 2024-2025 WCS DRC field observations.

Flagship Threat Index

The threat level for Forest Elephants is calculated based on the spatial overlap between historical poaching hotspots and current mining incursions.

03

Hectare Loss Calculation

Total forest loss is calculated by intersecting the annual MODIS Burned Area (MCD64A1) product with the University of Maryland Hansen GFC tree cover dataset.

Cumulative_Percent = (Sum(Annual_Forest_Loss_ha) / 1,372,625) * 100

The Cumulative Loss % is relative to the official reserve area of 1,372,625 hectares.
