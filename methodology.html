Scientific Methodology & Data Standards

1. Data Source Integration

The OWR Environmental Monitoring Platform utilizes a multi-sensor approach to quantify environmental changes. Our primary data streams include:

  • Hansen Global Forest Change (UMD): Annual forest loss quantification at 30m resolution.
  • MODIS (MCD64A1) & VIIRS (VNP14IMG): Thermal anomaly detection and burned area mapping.
  • Sentinel-2 (Copernicus): 10m resolution optical imagery for hotspot validation (Mining and Logging).
  • GEDI (NASA): Global Ecosystem Dynamics Investigation for above-ground biomass and carbon estimation.

2. Quantitative Metric Standards

All environmental impacts are calculated using standardized SI units to ensure scientific reproducibility:

Area (Hectares) 1 ha = 10,000 m². The primary unit for all forest loss and mining expansion metrics.
Area (Square Kilometers) 1 km² = 100 ha. Used for landscape-level reporting and regional statistics.
Carbon (CO2e) Calculated using GEDI-derived biomass density multiplied by a standard conversion factor (0.47-0.5) for carbon content.
Temporal Frequency Annual aggregates (2000-2026) with monthly alert frequencies for fire and logging.

3. Analysis Algorithms

We employ Parametric Change Detection to identify anomalies in spectral signatures indicative of forest degradation. Spatial Hotspot Clustering (using kernel density estimation) is applied to mining site detections to identify expansion fronts.
