The Methodology

Quantifying wildfire dynamics and forest loss through multi-sensor remote sensing integration (2001-2026).

1. Data Sources

Active Fire (FIRMS)

MODIS (MCD14DL) and VIIRS (VNP14IMGTDL) thermal anomalies provide near real-time and historical active fire locations. VIIRS offers higher spatial resolution (375m) compared to MODIS (1km).

Burned Area (MODIS)

The MCD64A1 Collection 6.1 Burned Area product is used to quantify the physical extent of fire damage in hectares.

Forest Loss (Hansen)

Global Forest Change (GFC) v1.9 data from the University of Maryland (UMD) enables high-resolution (30m) detection of tree cover loss.

3. Technical Limitations

  • Cloud Cover: Extensive cloud cover during the wet season may result in underestimation of active fire counts.

  • MODIS vs VIIRS: MODIS data (2001-present) is the primary long-term baseline, while VIIRS (2012-present) provides improved detection of small fires.
