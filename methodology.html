The Methodology

Advanced multi-criteria classification and severity modeling for wildfire dynamics in the Okapi Wildlife Reserve (2001-2026).

1. Fire Type Classification

Fires are classified into 9 distinct drivers using spatial proximity analysis, temporal recurrence, and spectral intensity indicators.

Slash-and-Burn

Detected in areas within 5km of villages with high forest loss intensity (>3ha) and high seasonality.

Mining-Related

Events intersecting or proximal to CAMI mining titles and IPIS artisanal site buffers.

Wildfire

Remote events (>10km from human features) with Extreme severity and dry season timing (Dec-Feb).

Charcoal Production

High forest loss events proximal to road corridors and transport hubs.

2. Severity Thresholds

Class FRP (MW) Burn Size (ha) Impact
Low < 30 < 2.0 Surface fire, low canopy impact
Moderate 30 - 60 2.0 - 4.0 Mixed intensity, understory loss
High 60 - 100 4.0 - 5.5 Significant crown impact
Extreme > 100 > 5.5 Complete stand replacement

3. Hectare Loss Calculation

Total forest loss is calculated by intersecting the annual MODIS Burned Area (MCD64A1) product with the University of Maryland Hansen GFC tree cover dataset. The Cumulative Loss % is relative to the official reserve area of 1,372,625 hectares.

Cumulative_Percent = (Sum(Annual_Forest_Loss_ha) / 1,372,625) * 100
Data integrated from NASA FIRMS & GFC v1.9
